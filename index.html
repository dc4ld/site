Datum Markets

We check what electricity really costs.

Businesses in southern Africa spend millions on power through bills and contracts that almost nobody independently verifies. Datum Markets rebuilds the numbers from the regulator's and the utility's own published documents — so buyers, lenders and traders know what power actually costs, and whether a promised saving is real.

What we do

Check the bill

We recompute an Eskom electricity bill line by line from the published tariff rules, and show exactly where the bill and the rules disagree.

Check the deal

Renewable power delivered over the grid — "wheeling" — is sold on claimed savings of 20–30%. We provide the independent benchmark that tests those claims, including the network charges the sales spreadsheets tend to leave out.

Make the market measurable

Southern Africa's traded electricity prices exist only inside PDF reports. We turn them into clean, usable data — and as South Africa's new wholesale market opens, we'll be measuring it from day one.

The 20% that isn't a discount

Eskom's generation capacity charge is commonly summarised as phased in at 20% then 30% — customers pay the full charge in every year, and no part of it can be reduced by wheeling.
