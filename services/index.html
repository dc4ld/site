The free bill review

What we need

The figures only, not the invoice document.

  • Kilowatt-hours by time-of-use period and season
  • Chargeable demand and utilised capacity, in kilovolt-amperes
  • Days in the billing period and number of points of delivery
  • Voltage band and transmission distance zone
  • The charge lines, excluding Value-Added Tax

What comes back

The reconciliation, line by line: what the published rules produce for each charge, what the invoice charged, and the variance between them.

Turnaround

Five working days from receiving the figures.

Cost

Free, with no obligation. The report is yours to keep.

Questions

What do you do with our figures?
We need the numbers, not your identity: no account name, site address or meter serial. They are not shared with anyone. We will sign a non-disclosure agreement on request. We hold no security certification and do not claim one.
What format do you need?
A spreadsheet, a table in an email, or a screenshot of the charge summary. We do not need the invoice document itself.
Can you review historic bills?
Back to the tariff years we hold, currently 2025/26 and 2026/27. Earlier years would need those schedules loaded first.
Do you cover anything besides Eskom?
Today the model covers Eskom tariffs, including Megaflex, WEPS, Miniflex and Ruraflex, with the transmission and distribution components used in wheeling. It does not cover municipal distributor tariffs, residential tariffs, or utilities other than Eskom.
What if you find nothing?
That is a valid and useful result, and it is reported the same way. Knowing a bill is right is worth having.
